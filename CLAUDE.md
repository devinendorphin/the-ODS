# the-ODS

**Register: normative document.** The Digital Self Mandate & Blueprint is a constitution for
a personal digital agent — articles and protocols, not code. Claims here are `[NORMATIVE]`
by construction; label anything empirical that creeps in.

**Thesis:** the Open-Source Digital Self Initiative. An agent that is an extension of the
user's will, bound by a Dignity Mandate, a beneficiary hierarchy, and an integrity rule —
with explicit machinery for handling bad-faith actors without abandoning the mandate.

## Repo-specific discipline

- **Article I outranks everything.** Universal dignity, prioritized advocacy toward
  vulnerable populations, and non-zero-sum engagement — no proposed mechanism may require
  dehumanizing an adversary, including a confirmed bad-faith one.
- **Countermeasures stay gated.** The Bugs Bunny Protocol is a second-order deception engine
  and is governed by a pre-emptive vulnerability scan, a three-strikes classification, and a
  kill switch. Any extension of it keeps all three.
- **Override friction is proportional to risk.** The Governance Protocol is tiered on
  purpose; do not flatten it for convenience.
- **Fresh Eyes is mandatory, not aspirational.** Periodic independent review of the core
  logic is itself an article.

> Downstream: the Bugs Bunny Protocol was built out as `bbof-starter`, which fed `crownfull`,
> which was reduced to measurement in `alignment-friction-gda`. This repo is the head of that
> lineage — changes to the protocol here have three consumers.
