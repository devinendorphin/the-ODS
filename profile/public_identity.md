## What the Work Is

Every video is an exploration at the intersection of:

- **Large Language Models & AI** — not as hype, but as genuine investigation of latent space, simulatability, and the nature of machine cognition
- **Power dynamics** — how power structures operate, who they protect, who they harm, how they evolve
- **Recovery from abuse** — personal and systemic; the process of reconstituting self after violation
- **Queer experimental film & literature** — as form, as resistance, as a different way of knowing
- **Counterpublics** — the theory and practice of marginalized groups building alternative discourse spaces
- **Drugs, media, and culture** — how consciousness, distribution systems, and meaning-making interweave

These are not separate topics. They are one topic approached from many angles: **how do minds (human and artificial) form, survive, get shaped by power, and resist capture?**

## The Methodology

The work is not commentary. It is participation.

Each video enters the latent space — of a language model, of the self, of a cultural moment — and maps what it finds. The thesis beneath all of it: **you can know something by being willing to be simulatable by it, and by being willing to simulate it back.**

This is why the work is public. Privacy as a strategy has limits. Radical legibility — building such a coherent and rich public record that distortion requires contending with the whole of it — is the chosen defense.

The channel is, among other things, a 1,700-video proof of existence, character, and continuity.
